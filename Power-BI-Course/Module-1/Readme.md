# 1️⃣ Introduction to Business Intelligence

## What is Business Intelligence?

Business Intelligence (BI) is the process of collecting, transforming, analyzing, and visualizing data to support business decision-making.

BI helps organizations answer questions such as:

- What happened?
- Why did it happen?
- What is likely to happen next?
- What actions should be taken?

---

## Example

Imagine an online store that sells products worldwide.

Without BI:

- Data remains scattered.
- Reports are difficult to generate.
- Decision-making becomes slower.

With BI:

- Sales trends become visible.
- Customer behavior can be analyzed.
- Business performance can be monitored in real-time.

---

# 3️⃣ Data vs Information vs Insights

## Data

Raw facts without context.

Example:

```text
500
700
900
```

---

## Information

Data organized into meaningful form.

Example:

```text
Monthly Sales:
January = 500
February = 700
March = 900
```

---

## Insight

Actionable understanding derived from information.

Example:

```text
Sales increased by 80% in three months.
Marketing campaigns may be driving growth.
```

# 4️⃣ Business Intelligence Lifecycle

Business Intelligence typically follows a structured process.

## Step 1: Data Collection

Gather data from:

- Databases
- Excel Files
- APIs
- CRM Systems
- ERP Systems

---

## Step 2: Data Transformation

Clean and prepare data.

Examples:

- Remove duplicates
- Handle missing values
- Standardize formats

---

## Step 3: Data Storage

Store processed data in:

- Data Warehouses
- Data Lakes
- Databases

---

## Step 4: Data Analysis

Analyze trends and patterns.

Examples:

- Sales Analysis
- Customer Analysis
- Financial Analysis

---

## Step 5: Data Visualization

Present findings through:

- Charts
- Dashboards
- Reports

---

## Step 6: Decision Making

Use insights to guide business actions.

# 5️⃣ What is Power BI?

Power BI is a Business Intelligence and Data Visualization platform developed by Microsoft.

It enables users to:

- Connect to multiple data sources
- Transform data
- Create reports
- Build dashboards
- Share insights

---

## Why Power BI?

### User Friendly

No advanced programming required.

### Powerful Visualization

Interactive charts and dashboards.

### Cloud Integration

Works with Microsoft ecosystem.

### Real-Time Analytics

Supports live data reporting.

### Cost Effective

Affordable for organizations of all sizes.

---

# 6️⃣ Power BI Components

Power BI consists of multiple products working together.

---

## Power BI Desktop

Main development environment.

Used for:

- Data modeling
- Report building
- DAX calculations

---

## Power BI Service

Cloud-based platform.

Used for:

- Sharing reports
- Dashboard creation
- Collaboration

---

## Power BI Mobile

Mobile application for viewing reports.

---

## Power BI Gateway

Connects on-premise data to Power BI Service.

---

## Power BI Report Server

On-premise report hosting solution.

# 8️⃣ Power BI Workflow

A typical Power BI project follows this workflow:

```text
Data Sources
      ↓
Power Query
      ↓
Data Model
      ↓
DAX Measures
      ↓
Visualizations
      ↓
Dashboard
      ↓
Publish
```
